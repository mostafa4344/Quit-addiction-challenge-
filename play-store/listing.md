## Full description (max 4000)

Quit Challenge helps you break a habit or addiction, one day at a time.

Pick what you want to quit (social media, mobile games, smoking, porn, junk food or anything else), set a goal in days or hours, and the app keeps your streak alive with a live timer, milestone badges and encouragement along the way.

WHEN AN URGE HITS
• Tap SOS, rate how strong the urge is, and ride it out with a short guided wave
• See your own reasons and your own if-then plan right when you need them
• Afterwards, see how much weaker the urge got. Urges pass.

SLIPS ARE NOT THE END
• A slip only restarts the current streak
• Your total clean time, best streak and badges stay
• Three quick questions after a slip help you spot the warning signs next time

KNOW YOUR PRESSURES
• Log what pulled you: stress, boredom, loneliness, tiredness and more
• Pressure insights show what pulls you most and a weekly map of your riskiest hours
• Write your own "if this happens, then I will…" plans

APP TIME LIMITS
• Choose apps and a daily limit, like Digital Wellbeing
• Get a break reminder near your limit and a full-screen "time for a break" screen
• Cross the limit and the challenge restarts, so it actually means something

STAY ON TRACK
• Daily check-in notifications at the times you choose: answer "still on track?" right from the notification
• Home screen widget with your streak
• See the time and money you have won back

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics
• The app has no internet permission: your challenges, slips and notes never leave your phone
• Optional PIN or fingerprint lock
• Back up and restore your data to a file you control

Quit Challenge is a self-help tool, not medical treatment. If you are struggling, please talk to a doctor or a local support service.
