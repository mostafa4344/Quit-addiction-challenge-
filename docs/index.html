What it does

⏱ Timed challenges

Set a goal in days or hours for social media, games, smoking, porn, junk food or anything else, and watch your streak grow.

🆘 SOS for urges

When an urge hits, rate it and ride it out with a short guided wave. Urges pass; the app shows you.

📊 Pressure insights

See which pressures pull you back (stress, boredom, late nights) and when, then write your own if-then plans.

📲 App time limits

Daily limits for chosen apps with break reminders and a full-screen "time for a break" screen.

📅 Daily check-ins

Answer "still on track?" right from a notification, at the times you choose.

🌱 Slips are not the end

A slip only restarts the streak. Your total clean time, best streak and badges stay.

🔒 App lock

Optional PIN or fingerprint lock, so nobody else can see your progress.

🏠 Home screen widget

Your streak at a glance, every time you unlock your phone.

How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded QuitChallenge.apk. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (only this once).
  3. Tap Install, then open Quit Challenge. The app walks you through the few permissions it needs and why.

Updates: download the newest version from this page and install it over the old one. Your data stays. Want to check the file? Its SHA-256 is on the release page.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. The app has no internet permission, so your challenges, slips and notes never leave your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Quit Challenge · Privacy policy · Help and feedback
