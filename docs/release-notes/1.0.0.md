## Quit Challenge 1.0.0

The first public version.

- Timed challenges for any habit: social media, games, smoking, porn, junk food or your own
- Live streak timer, progress ring, milestone badges and level-ups
- A slip only restarts the streak; total clean time, best streak and badges stay
- SOS urge help: rate the urge and ride it out with a guided wave
- Pressure insights: what pulls you back and when, plus your own if-then plans
- App time limits with break reminders and a full-screen break screen
- Daily check-in notifications at the times you choose
- Optional PIN / fingerprint app lock, home screen widget, backup and restore
- Private: no account, no ads, no internet permission; data stays on your phone

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.

**Install:** download `QuitChallenge.apk` below on your phone and open it. `QuitChallenge.apk.sha256` is its checksum. The `.aab` file is for Google Play.
